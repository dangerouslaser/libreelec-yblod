Live audio preroll candidate — 2026-10-10

Change
For realtime streams with a valid initial audio timestamp, initialize the
clock from actual buffered audio plus 100 ms headroom, rather than maximum
audio capacity plus 400 ms. Existing video timestamp constraint, unknown-PTS
fallback, non-realtime behavior and ongoing synchronization remain unchanged.
100 ms is an experimental safety margin, not a proven minimum for all feeds.
