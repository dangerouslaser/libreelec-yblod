These are per-frame sums of reconstruction and packing GPU kernel durations,
including fused movie-overlay composition. They exclude decode, GL skin
drawing, interop waits and scanout. They are NOT full playback latency and
must not be derived from, or directly compared with, GPU busy percentages.
24 fps allows 41.67 ms per frame, but excluded work also needs time.
This is one short sample per condition, not a distribution across whole movies.

Presentation and controls, profiling disabled
Selected P5, P8.1, P8.4, genuine P10.1 and P7 FEL scenes pass clean, controls-
open and controls-closed windows. Each window contains 101 unique successful
display commits, with no backwards PTS and maximum adjacent PTS gaps of
41.667 ms (P5/P8.4), 42.000 ms (P8.1), or 42.001 ms (P10.1/P7).
Pause, seek while paused, and resume pass on each selected scene.
P5/P8.1/P10.1 also ran sequentially in one process to exercise profile changes.
These warm-window checks do not establish cold-start behavior or every frame
of each supported profile. Missing genuine profile fixtures remain a limit.

Open issue: early P7 presentation
Short startup/seek-to-scene runs show occasional 83–84 ms adjacent committed
PTS gaps, with profiling both on and off and with controls both closed/open.
The missing frames were decoded and were pending for reconstruction/presentation
before a later frame was selected. A debug run logs a one-frame audio-clock
correction shortly before a skip. A short same-scene audio isolation test found
zero gaps over 50 ms with decoded PCM audio (maximum 42 ms), then two 83–84 ms
gaps after restoring passthrough. This implicates audio-clock synchronization
in the startup behavior, but does not yet establish the underlying timing error
or a fix. Passthrough remains enabled; disabling it is not an accepted solution.
Do not dismiss the gaps as profiler overhead or certify cold startup from the
passing later windows. Queue-skip counters alone do not capture every omission.

Packed-output reference checks
P8.4, P5 and P7 FEL each passed six 33,177,600-byte packed-frame comparisons
against the renderer's packing oracle. Selected tails reached their expected
final PTS and drained successfully with zero failed display commits. These
checks validate the packing path, not independent Dolby colour conformance or
every reconstructed sample. Readback perturbs cadence; these are not timing runs.
The first P7 harness run lacked its required FEL route diagnostic and failed
that assertion; rerunning with the route log enabled passed the unchanged gate.
