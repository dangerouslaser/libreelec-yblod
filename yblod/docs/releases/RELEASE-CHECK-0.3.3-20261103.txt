Changes since 0.3.2
- Linux 7.3: big-fella's i915/amdgpu HDMI patches from big-fella 69c770c (linux-yblod 7.3-7),
  byte for byte; every one applies with fuzz 0.
  - 9911 qms-hdr-8bpc (i915): QMS on HDR outputs whose 60 Hz base keeps the bpc.
  - 9912 hdmi-spd-sdi (i915): SPD source device "Digital STB" (i915.spd_sdi, 1; 9 = upstream's
    "PC general").
  - 9913 hdmi-scdc-recheck (i915): the sink's SCDC TMDS_Config is checked for 4 s after a hotplug,
    with a modeset if it doesn't match the link (at most 3 in 20 s).
  - 9923 hdr-yuv420 (amdgpu, i915): PQ/HLG at 4K50/60 as 10 bpc YCbCr 4:2:0 instead of 8 bpc RGB
    (amdgpu.hdr_yuv420, i915.hdr_yuv420). The DV tunnel stays 8 bpc RGB.
  - 9924 dcn10-black-bg (amdgpu): DCN 1.x's MPC background black in the output's colour space.
  - 9925 hdmi-hpd-hold (i915, amdgpu): an HPD drop of a sink with an active output is reported as
    connected for up to 3 s (i915.hpd_hold_ms; amdgpu.hdmi_hpd_debounce_delay_ms 3000).
  New, also in big-fella (e73aa82, linux-yblod 7.3-8): 9927 hdmi-scdc-after-hold (i915): no
  SCDC recheck while 9925 holds HPD low; HPD's return starts 9913's 4 s recheck.
  Not ported: 9926 qms-modes (off by default in big-fella; prunes 2160p23.976/29.97 on the G5).
- iPhone remote (yblod/iphone-remote, package yblod-iphone-remote): big-fella's big-fella-remote
  and bf-daemon crates (Apache-2.0), vendored from big-fella 12cf95b, with
  big-fella-remote-kodi (src/kodi.rs, written for this release): the pairing PIN as a Kodi
  notification; Kodi's keyboard dialog (Input.OnInputRequested/OnInputFinished) offered to the
  phone, its typing sent with Input.SendText, Return ends it. In the daemon, for it: the text
  event's "submit", return_key (off here: no Enter after Kodi's dialog), mdns_host (here
  <host>-<MAC end>-iphone-remote; Avahi has the host name).
  Services iphone-remote and iphone-remote-kodi (on by default; off with
  /storage/.cache/services/iphone-remote.disabled), settings from
  /storage/.config/iphone-remote.toml if present, state in /storage/.cache/iphone-remote.
  Programs > iPhone Remote (script.yblod.iphoneremote, an optional system add-on): pairing of
  new iPhones on or off, forget an iPhone, the remote off or on. TCP 49153 is open in the home
  firewall profile (private subnets), as every other port is there.

Testing (test images 0.3.3-test2, -test3 and -test3-lg; test2 differs from the release only in
the remote's start script and its settings template's comments)

- ryzen-fella (Ryzen 5 3500U, amdgpu DCN 1.0, LG B4):
  - HDR10 2160p59.94 (Gemini Man's base layer, DV stripped): the TV reports HDR10 YCbCr420.
    With amdgpu.hdr_yuv420=0: RGB.

    CRTC CRCs equal opaque black's, 9b83/9b83/5c03 (before 9924: b888/b888/c4c2).
  - DV 1917 (P7 FEL): 480 flips in 20 s (one per vblank), 8 bpc RGB, 0 drops, 0 skips.
  - iPhone remote, tests/e2e/kodi_iphone.py (pyatv as the iPhone, Kodi checked over JSON-RPC):
    found by an mDNS browse beside Avahi; PIN pairing; Home, a swipe + Select (Videos), Back and
    a volume button acted on by Kodi; Kodi's keyboard dialog brings up the phone's keyboard,
    "wörld ✓" typed on the phone reaches Kodi, Return ends the dialog with no Enter after it;
    the phone forgotten. Kodi restarting: the Kodi side reconnects by itself.
  - Programs > iPhone Remote, run with a scripted dialog: pairing off and on, the remote off
    (both services stopped) and on again. Kodi lists the add-on as enabled.
- N150 (i915, LG B4, its input not on screen: kernel side only):
  - HDR10 2160p59.94: port_clock 370878 (10 bpc 4:2:0).
  - DV 1917: 480 flips in 20 s, 0 drops.
- petunia (Intel i5-1135G7 in a VM with the iGPU, LG G5), 0.3.1-lg as the baseline:
  - The G5 reads the input's SPD as "Digital STB" (Intel, Integrated gfx).
  - 10 DV start/stops (1917 over NFS, the G5 sampled about 3 times a second): every HPD drop
    held ("HPD back after 1007-1027 ms"), 0 drm uevents (0.3.1-lg: 10 in 5 cycles), one SCDC
    modeset per stop after HPD's return, never "still wrong after 3 modesets". Blank on the
    screen: 0.9 s at a DV start (0.3.1-lg 0.9 s), 2.2 s after a DV stop (0.3.1-lg 1.8 s).
  - HDR10 2160p59.94: 10 bpc YCbCr 4:2:0 at 594 MHz, QMS 59.94 Hz on the 60 Hz base (9911).
  - DV 23.976 over QMS (base 60): 480 flips in 20 s, no half rate.

Limitations
- The iPhone remote was tested with pyatv playing the iPhone, not with a real iPhone on
  LibreELEC (big-fella's daemon has been used with a real iPhone).
- After a DV stop on the G5 the screen is blank about 0.4 s longer than in 0.3.1-lg: 9913's
  modeset after HPD comes back (the G5 reports TMDS_Config 0x00 then). No "No signal" was seen in
  15 stops with or without it.
- A real unplug (the output reported gone after 3 s) was not tested.
- QMS on the B4 (SDR 24p over QMS once showed corruption on the N150's B4, 2026-10-12) was not
  re-checked: QMS for SDR is unchanged from 0.3.2.
- The N150's 60p DV (about 40 fps shown) is unchanged from 0.3.2.
