- Boot on Linux 7.3.0: no i915/DRM errors; enable_dv_lab and qms parameters present; no Kodi
  errors at startup.
- 1917 (P7 FEL): play, seek 30:00, pause, paused seek +60 s, resume, seek back 10 min, PGS
  subtitles on/off, playback controls, Home over video, back to fullscreen, stop: all OK,
  0 Kodi errors. Signal dv=1, 3840x2160 23.976 Hz, RGB tunnel. 3 drops and 2 skips, only right
  after the back seek with subtitles on, as on 0.2.9-test1-lg (Linux 7.2.9).
- Engine GPU time, 60 s of 1917 from 30:00, DV_SOURCE_PROFILE=1, GPU clock pinned at RP0
  (1000 MHz), same box and session: 0.2.9-test1-lg (Linux 7.2.9) mean 12.32 ms, p95 12.45;
  0.2.9-test2-lg (Linux 7.3.0) mean 12.32 ms, p95 12.46; 1439 frames, 0 drops, 0 skips each.
- 0.2.9-test1-lg (the 2026-10-18 base) passed the same 1917 run.
