Findings, most important first
1. A flickering picture on the LG G5 with QMS on (the image's default), seen by the owner and not
   by anything on the box. During the 2026-11-19 run of the release images (LG edition, Intel Core
   i5-1135G7, LG G5), the owner, watching the TV, reported at about 06:47-06:49 that the picture
   was flashing and asked for playback to be stopped as a seizure risk; it was stopped at 06:49:36.
   Asked afterwards, the owner described it as a "rapid flicker on paused and playing picture"
   (the owner's observation, not a measurement). Conditions then: QMS on
   (/sys/module/i915/parameters/qms Y), the owner's own skin (Bald, heavier than Estuary), Kodi's
   Variable refresh rate off; 1917 TV-led Dolby Vision, playing (06:47:07-06:47:46 and from
   06:48:18) then paused from 06:48:38 at 40:03.979. At 07:10:12 the orchestrator wrote 0 to the
   qms parameter and switched the skin to Estuary YBLOD; 1917 then played TV-led from 34:00
   (07:11:12-07:11:36, 553 pictures, 0 dropped, 0 skipped in the kept kodi.log), and the owner
   reported "there is no flicker", and again during the agent's continuation that followed (TV-led,
   a paused frame, 2 min 50 s of player-led). QMS and the skin were changed together, and by
   decision nobody turned QMS back on to see the flicker return. So QMS is the likely cause, not an
   established one. Whether 0.5.1 flickers on this TV is not known.
   What the box showed (Hardware, run 3):
   - Kodi: no error about the picture from 06:48:18 to 06:49:37 (two "OutputPicture - timeout
     waiting for buffer" warnings at the seek and the pause), no "failed to configure renderer", 0
     dropped and 0 skipped. While paused under Bald, the engine redrew the same picture 24 times a
     second for the whole pause: frame serial 480 to 1800 at the same pts (2404110000) from
     06:48:39.2 to 06:49:34.3 (1320 pictures in 55 s), every 120-picture window "overlay prepare
     (120 of 120 frames)", "0 of 120 pictures written in part, 100.0% of the canvas", 12.34-12.39 ms
     GPU each; renderer closed processed=1845 held=1. Under Estuary the paused frame was held
     (held=104, 271, 113 in three later pauses), not redrawn. Candidate 2 also redrew a paused frame
     (run 1, the same pts on two consecutive lines at 18:41:33-18:41:38), so the redraw is not new;
     whether the redrawn pictures are identical was not checked.
   - Kernel: no HPD drop, modeset, underrun or link error in dmesg during the playback or the pause;
     the held HPD drop and SCDC modeset only at the output changes (06:47:48 after the previous stop,
     06:49:38 after the final stop). Connector sampled 100 times from 06:49:23.6 only: 45 samples
     2160p24 Dolby Vision (TUNNEL_FORMAT 0, Broadcast RGB full, Colorspace 0, no HDR metadata) to
     06:49:36.5, then the desktop from 06:49:36.6 (the stop); nothing changed before the stop.
   - TV: its report at about 06:48:4x and 60 reports from 06:49:23 to 06:49:26 only: Dolby Vision,
     frameRate 60, connected, in every one; the 06:48:4x report carries the QMS block (qmsVrrEnabled

   - What QMS changes in the signal (1917 TV-led, kept samples): with QMS on, the TV reports

   built, with the flicker documented as a known bug in the release notes and the documents

6. The GUI raster is shared with the engine on AMD (radeonsi, Mesa 26.3.0: "Private native GUI
   raster shared with the Vulkan engine" at every Dolby Vision start, 72 lines in the kept logs,
   never "no shared buffer") but not on the Tiger Lake VM (0.4.1 shared there, logging
   "modifier=0x100000000000002"). With kodi-9968 the release images log the failing step, verbatim
   (run 3, 06:39:15.741): "Private native GUI raster: no shared buffer (EGL image: eglCreateImageKHR
   error 0x3009 (modifier 0x2, stride 15360, offset 0)); the Vulkan engine copies it". 0x3009 is
   EGL_BAD_MATCH; Kodi's GUI plane is INTEL_Y_TILED and EGL lists
   EGL_EXT_image_dma_buf_import_modifiers. The hardware agent's inference, not checked: the modifier
   handed to EGL (0x2) lacks the Intel vendor prefix that 0.4.1's 0x100000000000002
   (I915_FORMAT_MOD_Y_TILED) has. No effect of the copy was measured. Unresolved.
7. Most userspace in the image was compiled before the kernel switch, against 7.3.4's headers (the
   0.6.0-dev1 build of ba8b701d6c, 334 of the 349 stamps). This is what the stamps allow (a
   package's stamp covers its own recipe, patches and PKG_NEED_UNPACK, not its dependencies), and no
   package whose inputs changed came from a stamp (Images). glibc and iptables were rebuilt against
   7.4's headers, ipset and wireguard-tools against the 7.4 kernel: every file of the four is
   byte-identical to 0.5.1's (built against 7.3). systemd, rebuilt against 7.4's headers, shows the
   effect where there is one: its systemd-journald knows the audit record type FSOPEN (AUDIT_FSOPEN,
   new in 7.4's linux/audit.h); its other 87 files are byte-identical to 0.5.1's. Neither hardware
   run found a failed unit, kernel error or call trace at boot.
8. dvvk-prepare is slower: 124.7 and 123.6 s from cold caches on Ollie (0.5.1's own SYSTEM, run the
   same way right after: 104.7 s), so about 19 s (18 %) more on every first boot after an update,
   from the release (Mesa 26.3.0's compiler or the engine's shaders; not separated). On the Ryzen
   5 3500U: 58 s, logged on both boots ("pipelines ready in 58 s"; 28 s for 0.5.0 there is reported
   but not in the kept logs). Intel run 1 reports 223 s (not in its kept logs); on the release
   images the same machine took 846 ms ("pipelines ready in 1 s", 695 ms CPU), reusing candidate
   2's cache (same engine, same Mesa). Warm: 0.68 s.
9. FreeSync on the AMD machine changed with 7.4 and linux-9925: the LG B4 now reads vrr_capable 1
   with a 40-120 Hz range, where 0.5.1 (7.3.0) read 0 with 0-0, and at the 0.6.0 menu the encoder
   sends an AMD FreeSync SPD (it is not sent during the Dolby Vision tunnel). This is a change on
   that machine, not continuity. Kodi's Variable refresh rate setting (off by default) is shown
   only on a VRR display, so B4 owners on AMD will now see it. FreeSync was not engaged (Kodi's VRR
   setting was off), and what the TV does with the SPD was not seen. Whether 0.5.1 sent an SPD is
   not in the logs (its registers were not read).
