YBLOD 0.4.1 validation

Build source: main at 3d947249fb (tree d22aed1c). The images were built from a66a99ea16, the head
of PR #35, whose tree is identical (BUILD_ID a66a99ea16a2b027c16688ef32b1939038b1bcb1). Changes
since 0.4.0 (1d020cad):
- PR #33: yblod-dv-prepare.service, dv-prepare and dvvk-prepare: the Vulkan engine's pipeline cache
  is built at boot (idle priority, after Kodi) once per image and GPU.
- PR #34: even picture sizes. Engine: source_geometry and the metadata parser take even unscaled
  sizes (multiples of 4 kept for scaled pictures and, in plan.c, with an EL); dvvk-bench places
  pictures on even sizes. Kodi yblod-35: placement rounded to an even row/column, SourceEligible
  takes even unscaled single-layer sizes, and the eligibility check at file open rejects sizes the
  engine cannot take (wider than 3840, odd, Profile 7 not a multiple of 4) so they play as the
  base layer.
- PR #35: the prepare service is wanted by kodi.target (under multi-user.target, After=kodi.service
  was an ordering cycle and systemd dropped the job at boot); yblod-35 also crops a scaled
  single-layer picture to multiples of 4 (1920x1038 -> 1920x1036 in a 3840x2072 rectangle).

Images
Both editions built in the release tree on Ollie (run-release.sh 0.4.1, incremental on 0.4.0's
tree). SYSTEM compared with 0.4.0 file by file (standard edition): nothing removed; added
yblod-dv-prepare.service and its kodi.target.wants link, /usr/lib/yblod/dv-prepare and
dvvk-prepare; changed kodi.bin, libdv_engine_vk.so.1 and version stamps (os-release, issue,
release, defs.pyc, a generated slice unit, /usr/cache/shadow). KERNEL identical to 0.4.0.

Testing
- Engine (Ollie, UHD 770): cargo test 38/38; golden.sh identical (102 hashes) and FLIP_Y twins
  identical (18): output for the 3840x2160 reference clips (1917 FEL, Gemini Man MEL, 28 Years
  Later P8.1) is unchanged. The geometry tests cover 3840x1746, 3840x1606, 1920x1038 and 3834x1600
  (accepted), odd placement and odd sizes (rejected), scaled sizes not a multiple of 4 (rejected).
- Test clips: 28 Years Later P8.1 cropped with x265 and its RPU re-injected with dovi_tool 2.3.1.
  - 0.4.0's engine refuses 3840x1746 (12/12 pictures); 0.4.1's renders it.
  - Lossless crops at 3840x1746 and 3840x1744 placed at row 206: identical pixels apart from the
    metadata rows (0-1) and two rows where the clip's L5 boundary moves with the height.
  - Picture edges (L5 set to the full frame, crops where picture reaches the edge): 3834x1438 and
    3832x1436 (already supported) against a 3840x1440 reference both differ only in the last 4
    rows (the vertical chroma filter at the edge); right edge and interior identical.
- Hardware: Petunia (Tiger Lake VM, LG G5 on HDMI 1), LG edition, this release's image:
  - 3840x1746 (7 s) Dolby Vision, 169/169 pictures presented; 1920x1038 (scaled to 4K) 169/169;
    3834x1438 (scaled) 17/17; 28 Years Later P8.1 3840x2160 170/170; 1917 P7 FEL 30 s, 692/692.
    No drops, skips or refused pictures; Dolby Vision signalled (dv=1) for each.
  - The first 0.4.1 build refused 1920x1038 and 3834x1438 (scaled pictures) and its prepare
    service was dropped by the ordering cycle; both fixed in PR #35 and re-tested above.
  - yblod-dv-prepare at boot with the engine and Mesa caches deleted: started with Kodi, pipelines
    ready in 54 s (52.6 s CPU at idle priority), marker written; with a valid cache 1 s. The first
    picture of each title above took 48-83 ms.
- NOT tested: the standard edition on a TV (same Kodi and engine as the LG edition), a real
  cropped release file (the test clips are re-encodes of a P8.1 clip), Profile 5 at a cropped size,
  the N150 and AMD.
